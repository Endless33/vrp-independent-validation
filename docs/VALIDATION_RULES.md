# Validation Rules

**Document Version:** Public v1

**Status:** Public Engineering

---

# Purpose

This document defines the engineering rules for participating in the VRP Independent Validation Program.

Its objective is to ensure that published validation results remain reproducible, objective, and technically meaningful.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

Independent validation should evaluate engineering behavior.

It should not become a marketing exercise.

Every conclusion should be supported by observable engineering evidence.

---

# Rule 1 — Preserve Objectivity

Participants should document what was observed.

Not what was expected.

Engineering observations are more valuable than assumptions.

---

# Rule 2 — Preserve Original Evidence

Original evidence should remain unchanged.

Representative evidence includes:

- execution logs
- manifests
- benchmark outputs
- validation reports
- environment information

Engineering evidence should accurately reflect the executed validation.

---

# Rule 3 — Document The Environment

Every report should identify the evaluation environment, including:

- operating system
- hardware
- virtualization platform
- software versions
- relevant dependencies

Reproducibility depends on environmental transparency.

---

# Rule 4 — Separate Facts From Interpretation

Reports should clearly distinguish:

Observed engineering behavior.

Engineering analysis.

Personal opinion.

Only observed behavior should be treated as engineering evidence.

---

# Rule 5 — Report Unexpected Results

Unexpected behavior should never be hidden.

Unexpected observations may improve future engineering understanding.

Both successful and unsuccessful validation contribute value.

---

# Rule 6 — Respect Evaluation Boundaries

Participants should not disclose:

- protected runtime implementation
- confidential engineering information
- proprietary customer information
- material protected by agreement

Independent validation should remain lawful and ethical.

---

# Rule 7 — Encourage Independent Reproduction

Engineering conclusions become stronger when multiple organizations independently reproduce similar results.

Participants are encouraged to describe their methodology clearly enough for others to repeat.

---

# Rule 8 — Publish Honest Conclusions

Validation reports should describe the engineering outcome accurately.

Possible conclusions include:

- reproduced
- partially reproduced
- additional investigation required
- not reproduced under documented conditions

Each conclusion should be supported by engineering evidence.

---

# Final Principle

Engineering credibility comes from reproducibility.

Independent validation should prioritize transparency, evidence, and technical integrity over expectations or conclusions.
