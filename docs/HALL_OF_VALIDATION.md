# Hall of Independent Validation

**Document Version:** Public v1

**Status:** Public Engineering

---

# Purpose

This document records organizations, engineering teams, research laboratories, universities, and independent engineers that have completed an independent technical evaluation of VRP and agreed to have their participation publicly acknowledged.

Its objective is to demonstrate reproducible engineering validation performed by parties other than the project author.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

Independent engineering validation is one of the strongest forms of technical evidence.

A protocol should not become trusted because its creator says it works.

Confidence increases when multiple independent participants reproduce published engineering behavior.

---

# Publication Principles

Participation in this document is entirely voluntary.

Organizations may choose to:

- publish under their company name
- publish under an engineering team
- publish under an individual engineer
- publish anonymously (where appropriate)

No participant is required to disclose confidential information.

---

# Validation Status

The following status values may be used.

## Validation Completed

The participant completed an independent engineering evaluation and published a report.

---

## Validation In Progress

The participant is currently performing an engineering evaluation.

No engineering conclusions should be drawn until the evaluation is complete.

---

## Validation Published

The participant has chosen to publicly share engineering observations and supporting evidence.

---

## Additional Investigation

The participant identified observations requiring further engineering analysis.

Further validation may be recommended.

# Recognition

Participation in this program does not imply:

- certification
- endorsement
- commercial partnership
- regulatory approval

It recognizes only that an independent engineering evaluation has been performed.

---

# Engineering Value

Every independently published report contributes to a broader engineering understanding of VRP.

Reports that reproduce published behavior are valuable.

Reports identifying unexpected behavior are equally valuable.

Engineering improves through reproducible observation.

---

# Future Growth

As additional organizations participate, this document will become a historical record of independent engineering validation across different environments, industries, and evaluation teams.

---

# Final Principle

The strongest engineering confidence is earned when independent organizations reach similar conclusions through their own testing.

Engineering should always remain evidence-driven.

This document exists to recognize that process.
